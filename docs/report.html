Authorized admin activity

A password-retry sequence, encoded maintenance script and approved upload produce three false-positive alerts. Ground-truth approval is not supplied to the detector.

Quiet compromise

A valid-credential login has no failure burst. Its malicious shell and transfer are missed by R2/R3. Gating every downstream alert on R1 reduces coverage.

Metrics use rule/anchor pairs in a small designed dataset. R1 truth covers malicious burst logins; R2/R3 truth includes the quiet malicious shell/transfer. These are not population-level detection rates.
